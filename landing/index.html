NYC Subway Scheduling Optimization

AI-driven demand prediction and headway optimization for the Metropolitan Transportation Authority — reducing passenger wait times while managing operational costs.

Station 01

The Problem

The MTA subway system serves 3.6 million riders daily across 36 lines and 472 stations. Fixed-schedule timetables ignore fluctuating passenger demand, weather disruptions, and special events — leading to overcrowding during peaks and wasteful empty runs during off-hours.

The Method

A two-stage pipeline: demand prediction using gradient-boosted models trained on historical ridership, weather, and calendar features, followed by headway optimization via constrained quadratic programming — balancing passenger wait times against fleet operational costs through a tunable λ parameter.

Station 03

The Results

On tested routes, the optimized schedule reduces average passenger wait time by 18–32% compared to fixed GTFS timetables. During peak hours, headways dynamically tighten; during off-peak, trips are consolidated — cutting overcrowding risk while controlling fleet costs.
